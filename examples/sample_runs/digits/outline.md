# Outline — Training Budget Sets the No-Augmentation Control for an MLP on 8x8 Digits, and 3-Seed Estimates Overstate It

**Question this paper answers:** The question this paper answers is: which no-augmentation MLP control must a sub-pixel augmentation arm beat on sklearn 8x8 digits, and how far do the 3-seed estimates that selected that control shift when re-measured at 5 seeds?  
**Nugget:** Configs that won at 3 seeds regressed by 0.31-0.42 points at 5 seeds. The best control (hidden=256, wd=1e-3, 360 epochs, exp_0016) sits 0.53 points below the 3.8 baseline at 3.2712 val, which is 1.6 SE (SE 0.32 from 5 seeds), but its per-seed std of 0.72 exceeds that margin. Its single held-out test score is 3.3333. An augmentation arm must therefore be scored against this measured control, not against 3.8.

**Contributions**

- A seed-shift audit of the three configs promoted after winning at 3 seeds. From 3-seed verify to 5-seed full, means rose by 0.37 (2.8968→3.2712, exp_0015→exp_0016), 0.42 (3.1286→3.5497, exp_0008→exp_0009) and 0.31 (3.2444→3.5494, exp_0002→exp_0003). One 3-seed std of 0.0063 became 0.9374 at 5 seeds. We read this as selection-induced regression to the mean, scoped to these 3 configs, and not as a general bias of 3-seed runs.
- A long-budget no-augmentation control. hidden=256, wd=1e-3, lr=0.05, depth 1, 360 epochs gives 3.2712 ± 0.7247 std val over 5 seeds (margin 0.53 = 1.6 SE below 3.8) and a once-scored held-out test error of 3.3333 (exp_0016). The other 5-seed configs sit only 0.6-0.7 SE below baseline: 3.5494 ± 0.833 val / 3.7222 test (240 ep, exp_0003) and 3.5497 ± 0.9374 val / 3.5 test (hidden=128, 300 ep, exp_0009).
- A scoped budget trend at hidden=256, wd=1e-3. Within the smoke stage, error is non-increasing from 120 to 360 epochs (4.1667, 3.8194, 3.4722, 3.4722, 3.125). Within the verify stage, it is non-increasing from 240 to 360 epochs (3.2444, 3.2444, 3.1282, 2.8968). Each verify step lies within the larger of the two arms' stds. At 5 seeds, 240 vs 360 epochs (3.5494 ± 0.833 vs 3.2712 ± 0.7247) is not separated.
- No width or decay effect resolved within 3-seed spread at 240-300 epochs. hidden 128 vs 256 gives 3.3601 vs 3.2444 at 240 ep and 3.1286 vs 3.2444 at 300 ep. wd 5e-4 vs 1e-3 at 300 ep gives 3.244 ± 0.5261 vs 3.2444 ± 0.1974.
