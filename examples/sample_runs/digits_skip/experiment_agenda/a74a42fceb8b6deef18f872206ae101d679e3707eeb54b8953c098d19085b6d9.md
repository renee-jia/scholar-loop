## Limitations

- The frozen scorer only builds Linear/ReLU stacks, so residual arms are scored through an exactly equivalent width-256 re-expression of the trained 128-wide residual weights. The trained parameter set is still 42,634 tensors in both arms; the exported zeros and identity blocks are not trainable parameters. Equivalence is asserted in-script, but float summation order may flip a tie on rare images.
- The reference (unmodified default: full-batch, depth 1) differs from the arms in depth and batching. It serves only as the runner's anchor. The arm-vs-arm contrast must be read as a difference of paired differences.
- The engine computes only a descriptive paired mean difference. The pre-registered TOST and bootstrap CIs on best-over-steps and AUC are not computed here.
- There are 20 seeds rather than the registered 30, and the validation fold has about 288 images, so one image is about 0.35 pp. The ±0.5 pp margin is close to the resolution limit.
- Validation curves per epoch are unavailable because train.py never sees the val split. Speed is proxied by the training-set loss and error logged every 10 epochs, so final versus best-over-steps validation error cannot be separated.
- The idea leaves open whether the input projection is followed by a ReLU. This design uses a ReLU, matching the frozen build_model; this is also what makes the residual stream nonnegative.
- The training-step budget, prompts/data, weight decay, momentum, batch size and epochs are identical across all four arms. Only SKIP and lr differ, and lr is shared within each pair.
